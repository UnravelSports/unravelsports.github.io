UnravelSports

Sports Data Analytics Research & Engineering Consultancy

UnravelSports is an independent sports data analytics consultancy founded by Joris Bekkers, specializing in analytics research, technical consulting, strategic consulting, and software engineering, with nearly 10 years of experience in cutting-edge football analytics working with on-ball event data, positional tracking data, and skeletal tracking data.

UnravelSports consults for sports organizations, startups, and data providers to develop advanced analytics solutions, production-ready software, research and development and to offer insights into all aspects of the football analytics landscape.
